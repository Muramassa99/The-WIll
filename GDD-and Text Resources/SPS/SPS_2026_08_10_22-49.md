## What Is

Everything below exists in the current working tree but is intentionally dirty,
unstaged, uncommitted, and unpushed so it can be manually tested and adjusted.

### Menu workflow

- Opening Draft, Build, Material, Shape, Profiles, Layers, View, Status, or
  Settings closes the previously open top-level popup tree.
- Profiles and Settings replace one another, and top menus close either major
  workspace when deliberately selected.
- Selecting a saved Basic profile through
  `Shape -> 2D Profiles -> Saved Profiles` applies it and closes the Shape tree.
- Alt-tab/focus loss does not close major workspaces. Temporary RMB menus keep
  their prior hierarchy.
- `How To Do Menu Work.md` was not updated because interactive approval of the
  new feel is still required.

### Volume Stroke smoothing and live preview

- Smoothness `0` keeps direct input behavior.
- Smoothness `1..10` now uses a bounded, fixed-cadence screen-space stabilizer.
  Higher values trail the cursor and use more motion history.
- The buffered tail drains on release and emits the exact final valid endpoint.
- Stabilized points are raycast after smoothing, preserving actual surface hits
  and paired surface normals rather than smoothing a chord through the object.
- Appends are batched. Focus loss and all normal finish paths restore accumulated
  input ownership.
- Live drawing uses a collisionless swept `ArrayMesh` rather than rebuilding
  active CSG for every accepted point. Saved Basic polygons, contact frame,
  rotation bias, surface normals, material color, and red VOID preview are
  represented.

### Committed volume and release work

- The material ledger no longer performs the previous repeated full before,
  after, rebuild, and immediate status-resolution sequence.
- A transient bounded committed-occupancy cache reuses the current voxel basis
  and evaluates only newly appended bodies when safe.
- A sample-basis/history change performs one authoritative rebuild, then becomes
  reusable again. Cache state resets across load, migration, undo, and redo.
- Ledger schema/migration and zero-entry cleanup were updated so old persisted
  totals cannot remain authoritative under the new Handle policy.
- This substantially reduces project-script work, but final native static CSG
  and collision rebuilding is still synchronous/deferred Godot work and may
  still hitch. Interactive timing is required before claiming the reported
  10-20 second release freeze is solved.

### Protected Handle authority

- Handle authority is determined by material-body kind, not appearance,
  material choice, or the currently selected tool.
- Handle cells cannot be replaced or removed by ordinary material or VOID.
- Incoming material may attach and occupy empty space outside the protected
  Handle; the Handle keeps authority where they overlap.
- Handle bodies normalize to Add plus Replace as their own authored operation.
- The material-volume resolver and visual CSG clipping use the same composition
  policy. Detail material does not expand grip/Handle semantics.

### Detailing Brush

- A distinct `Detailing Brush` tool now exists beside free Spline Line.
- The first control point locks either the base authoring plane or one connected
  committed-material surface target. Later add/drag operations must hit that
  exact target; misses and target changes keep the last valid state.
- Controls, normals, target identity, dense resolved path, dense normals,
  per-span validity/reasons, and selected point are updated as one transaction.
- Control dots are pass-through constraints. The shared bounded sampler and
  surface resolver produce a dense path that remains on the locked surface.
- Invalid spans remain visible in red and disable Generate. They are not silently
  bridged, rerouted, or moved to the base plane.
- Generated Detail bodies use the persisted dense points and normals as a
  linear capsule/profile path. The final body is not smoothed through another
  spline and cannot reinterpret the path on WIP reload.
- Primitive and saved Basic cross-sections are supported. Saved-profile red-dot
  anchoring, 0.5 mm contact clearance, per-point normals, and rotation bias are
  retained.
- Free Spline Line remains its existing free structural mode.
- Initial policy is deliberately strict: one Detail path cannot jump voids or
  cross to a different connected target ID, including a separate material-zone
  seam. A future seam rule requires explicit evidence and a separate decision.

## What The User Should Do Next

Manual testing is now the next authority. Do not begin speculative tuning before
capturing a concrete failing shape/path.

1. Open Forge V2 and confirm deliberate top-menu replacement, Profiles/Settings
   replacement, Shape closure after saved-profile selection, and alt-tab
   persistence.
2. Test Volume Stroke Smoothness at `0`, a middle value, and `10` on a flat
   plane and a curved/changing-normal surface. Check cursor lag, final endpoint,
   jitter reduction, wrapping, and live frame time.
3. Release short and long saved-profile strokes. Record the post-release wait
   and Godot frame-time spike. Capture profile, path length, point count, and
   target shape for any remaining severe hitch.
4. Select Detailing Brush. Place controls on the base plane, then on one curved
   committed object. Confirm controls are pass-through points, the line remains
   surface-bound, and saved Basic profile orientation follows the surface.
5. Attempt to drag/add off the locked object or across a void. The prior valid
   control must remain and an invalid span must be red/non-generatable.
6. Generate Detail over ordinary material and over a Handle. Confirm Detail is
   visible, Handle material/geometry remains protected, and VOID cannot carve
   or shorten the Handle.
7. Save/reload a Forge WIP containing unfinished and committed Detail work.
   Confirm controls, target, dense path, normals, material, layer, and generated
   result survive without changing shape.
8. Compare live Detail preview with the committed static CSG on tight curves.
   Capture any twist, snap, penetration, or visual mismatch.
