### Digit hinge authority

  the 0.5/0.4/0.3 mm section targets with +/-0.08 mm target tolerance.

### Single-hand three-slice weapon seat

- `axial_reposition_offset` selects the Handle station through the existing
  ratio/path system.
- The solver samples:
  - C0: selected grip slice center;
  - Ci: Handle slice center at the Index1 axial station;
  - Cp: Handle slice center at the Pinky1 axial station.
- The hand and all hand bones are read-only during this seat step.
- Index1 and Pinky1 are the only positioning/rotation authorities.
- The weapon receives:
  - a perpendicular/radial C0 translation;
  - the shortest-arc Index/Pinky chord rotation.
- The selected axial station cannot slide, and the solve cannot introduce an
  independent Handle-axis roll. Authored tip/pommel source fields remain owned
  by the macro editor.
- The original `boundary radius + skin radius` estimate was found incorrect for
  oblique/irregular faces. It could leave a proximal bone sphere 0.5-2.5 mm
  inside the Handle even though the center ray appeared correct.
- `player_hand_surface_seat_solver.gd` revision v3 now:
  - keeps the first center-ray boundary as the radial branch anchor;
  - brackets outward on that same authorized radial;
  - bisects exact nearest point-to-Handle-surface distance;
  - targets `nearest surface distance == calibrated skin radius`, meaning exact
    zero-overlap tangency before digit curling;
  - confirms the authority point is outside the solid;
  - rejects authority penetration beyond 0.5 mm even if positional error is
    otherwise inside the public +/-1 mm seat band.
- Index/Pinky remain the only production seat authorities. No Middle/Ring
  steering inputs were invented.

### Lifecycle boundary

- Skill Crafter editor entry attempts one exact seat and digit solve after the

- A successful seat is cached in weapon-grip-local space as a full Transform3D.
- Macro tip/pommel movement, ordinary macro refresh, manual roll, and debug
  visibility reuse/recompose the cached seat; they do not launch another exact
  geometry solve.
- Debug toggling is visibility-only and must not move the weapon or fingers.
- Exact digit solving is now called only after a valid applied single-hand seat.
- Two-hand states are explicitly deferred by this new single-hand seat path;
  they do not reuse or apply this unfinished policy.
