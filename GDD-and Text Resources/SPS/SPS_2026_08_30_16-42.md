## Cause Found

The direct arm writer and elbow visual treated both elbows as the same unsigned
positive range. A focused baseline probe produced:

- right local-X bend: `+53.735591 degrees`
- left local-X bend: `+58.601276 degrees`

That matched the reported failure: both sides bent in the same signed local
direction even though only the left side was visually correct.
