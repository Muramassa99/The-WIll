Existing point dragging, Space/Shift+Space and twist controls edit a detached
source. S offers Apply/Cancel. Escape cancels to selection, then exits selection;
it does not close Forge. E exit and Q tool changes use the same unfinished-work
gate. The deposition ball stays hidden throughout selection/editing.

Apply reconstructs an independent native backend from S0 plus at most five
ordinary operations. Existing geometry/collider stays authoritative until the
candidate is ready and current. Successful publication preserves source/layer
IDs, chronological age and accounting; failure retains the old solid and draft.
Local Undo works inside the edit. Apply is one global Undo action. Mixed held
Undo/Redo batches replay once on a separate bounded state. Promotion prunes
actions dependent on an owner that becomes fused. Save applies an active edit
before saving. No C++ or DLL changes.

Saved-profile splines retain their saved polygon, anchor and twist. Exact-zero
float32 export facets are removed and exact duplicate checkpoint positions welded
before native validation, without moving geometry or relaxing tolerances.

Selection reads original mesh vertex/index arrays. Godot TriangleMesh queries
use millimetres internally to avoid its default coordinate rounding distorting
small faces. A full final triangle must fit one source triangle; newer partial
overlap blocks older fallback. Ambiguous merged faces remain red. This map is
rebuilt on selection/publication changes, not every pointer move.
